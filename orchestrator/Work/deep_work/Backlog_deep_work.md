# Guiding Principle

> Don't organize deep work by urgency.

Organize it by **why it deserves your deepest attention**.

- 🔴 Reduce pain.
- 🟡 Create opportunity.
- 🔵 Become who you want to be.
- 🟢 Serve the people who matter most.
